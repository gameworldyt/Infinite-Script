# InfiniteScript Trademarks & Branding

"InfiniteScript" and "Infinite UI" are project names and may be protected as
trademarks or other brand identifiers where applicable.

## You may

- Refer to InfiniteScript when discussing the project.
- Identify an unmodified official release as InfiniteScript.
- Identify a fork as a fork of InfiniteScript, provided it is clearly labelled.

## You may not

Without permission, you may not:
- Present a fork as the official InfiniteScript project.
- Use InfiniteScript branding to imply endorsement.
- Register confusingly similar project names.
- Copy official branding in a way that causes users to believe your project is
  the official release.

## Forks

If you create a fork, clearly state that it is an independent fork and identify
the original InfiniteScript project.
